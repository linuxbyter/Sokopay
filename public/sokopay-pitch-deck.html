01
The Problem

Kenya's informal economy is
invisible to digital consumers

🏪

No digital presence

Millions of micro-vendors — mama mbogas, barbers, eateries, boda riders — have zero online visibility. Customers find them only by walking past.

💸

Untracked transactions

Every transaction is cash, unrecorded, unverified. No payment history means no credit profile. No credit profile means no financial services.

📵

Broken discovery

Existing platforms (Google Maps, social media) require technical literacy that most informal vendors don't have, and don't support real-time availability.

30%+ of Kenya's GDP comes from the informal sector — operating entirely without digital infrastructure.

04
3-Phase Strategy

Free today. Payment rails tomorrow.
Credit network after that.

Phase 1 — Now
🏪

The Moat

Free marketplace. Zero fees for vendors or customers. Goal: 500 DAU in Nairobi. Build the liquidity — buyers and sellers who depend on the platform daily.

Building now
Phase 2 — V2
💰

SökoPay Payments

In-app escrow. Customer pays SökoPay, vendor gets funds on confirmation. M-Pesa integration. We take 1–2% per transaction. Every informal sale now flows through our rails.

Post 500 DAU
Phase 3 — V3
🌍

SökoPay Credit

Buy-now-pay-later for daily essentials. Chapati, water, haircut, gas — pay Friday. Credit scoring from transaction history. Klarna for Kenya's informal sector.

The endgame
💡

Why this works: Phase 1 builds the transaction history that makes Phase 3 possible. No credit bureau has data on informal vendors. We will. That data moat is worth more than the marketplace itself.

05
Business Model

Free to grow. Revenue from
the financial layer.

🆓

V1 — Free

Zero revenue. 100% focused on user acquisition and network effects. Cost: hosting (~$50/mo on Vercel + Neon free tier).

💳

V2 — Transaction fees

1.5% per transaction via in-app escrow. At 500 DAU × 2 transactions/day × KES 500 avg = KES 7,500/day (~$58).

📈

V3 — Credit interest

BNPL at 5–8% monthly interest on micro-credit (KES 200–5,000 ticket size). Underwritten by in-app transaction history. Highest margin product.

Why 1.5% is a no-brainer for vendors

  • M-Pesa charges 0–1.5% on withdrawals anyway
  • We provide dispute protection, ratings, and reach

  • We build their credit profile for Phase 3 financing
